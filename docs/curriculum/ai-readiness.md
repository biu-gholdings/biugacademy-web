# AI readiness track

## Status

**Planned expansion** — literacy-focused, not production AI engineering certification.

## Learning objectives (planned)

- Understand what AI tools can and cannot do reliably
- Use AI assistively for learning and productivity with verification habits
- Recognize bias, privacy, and misinformation risks
- Prepare for future-of-work changes without hype

## Boundaries

- Does not imply deployment of Academy-operated AI tutors in production today
- Does not certify learners as professional ML engineers without a separate, explicit program design
- No speculative token or investment content

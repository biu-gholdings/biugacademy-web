# Content style guide

## Language priority

| Order | Language | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| 1 | Portuguese (Angola) | Primary for public pages and curriculum |
| 2 | English | Secondary; maintain semantic parity with PT |
| 3 | French | Later; update when quality review available |

## Tone

- Clear and direct
- Practical and institutional
- Angola-first framing
- Respectful of learner dignity

## Avoid

- Exaggerated economic claims (“trillion,” “guaranteed wealth”)
- Speculative token or crypto promotion
- Implied government endorsement
- Hype adjectives without substance (“revolutionary,” “world-changing”)
- False urgency unrelated to real cohort deadlines

## Regulatory phrases

When describing the Academy, include or link to standard disclosure:

> BIU.G Academy is not a university and does not currently confer accredited academic degrees. Programs are under development with attention to Angola’s educational guidance frameworks.

## Formatting

- Short paragraphs and scannable headings
- Use lists for steps and criteria
- Tables only when they improve clarity

## Translation workflow

1. Draft in Portuguese
2. Translate to English with glossary consistency
3. French only after PT/EN stabilizes

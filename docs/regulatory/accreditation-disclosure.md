# Accreditation disclosure

## Public statement

**BIU.G Academy does not currently confer accredited academic degrees.**

Participation in planned or future learning activities, waitlists, or community programs does not constitute enrollment in an accredited higher education institution unless explicitly stated in a future, legally valid instrument.

## Future program development

Curriculum and credentials are **under development**. Any future certificate or recognition model will be described accurately—including whether it is:

- A completion certificate from BIU.G Academy (non-degree), or
- A pathway aligned with external accredited institutions (only if formally established and disclosed)

## Website consistency

The public site includes a regulatory notice on primary locale pages. Documentation and marketing must remain aligned with that notice.

## Questions

Contact [support@biugacademy.org](mailto:support@biugacademy.org). Do not interpret informal communications as accreditation promises.

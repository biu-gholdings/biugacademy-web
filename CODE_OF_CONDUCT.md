# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

BIU.G Academy aims to be a respectful, practical learning community—in public issues, pull requests, contributor channels, and any future cohort forums. We prioritize safety, honesty, and educational integrity.

This code applies to all participants in project spaces and to anyone representing the initiative in public.

---

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and professionalism
- Give constructive feedback focused on learning outcomes and documentation quality
- Use accurate language about credentials, programs, and regulatory status
- Respect privacy; do not share others’ personal information without consent
- Welcome contributors from diverse backgrounds and experience levels

---

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation of any kind
- Sexual attention or advances in community spaces
- Exploitation of learners, applicants, or contributors (financial, emotional, or labor)
- **False credential claims** — misrepresenting degrees, licenses, accreditation, or official titles
- **Political manipulation** — using Academy spaces for partisan campaigning unrelated to education mission
- **Financial promises** — guaranteed income, returns, employment, or investment outcomes
- Spam, scraping abuse, or deliberate disruption of intake systems
- Publishing others’ private data (doxxing)

---

## Educational integrity

- Do not instruct learners to bypass laws, regulations, or institutional policies
- Do not present speculative products (tokens, investments) as Academy-endorsed offerings unless formally documented and approved by maintainers
- Flag inaccurate or harmful content through issues or maintainer contact

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, moderate, or remove participants who violate this code. Serious violations may result in permanent exclusion from contribution and cohort participation.

Report concerns to: [support@biugacademy.org](mailto:support@biugacademy.org)

For security vulnerabilities, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (do not disclose exploit details publicly).

---

## Scope

This conduct code governs repository collaboration and community interaction. It does not replace applicable law in Angola or other jurisdictions.

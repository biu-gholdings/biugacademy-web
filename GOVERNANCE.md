# Governance

Lightweight governance for an initiative **under development**. This framework prioritizes **discipline**, **transparency**, and **educational integrity** over rapid feature expansion.

---

## Founder-led structure (current)

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Founder & Executive Director | Strategic direction, public representation, final merge authority |
| Maintainers | Documentation accuracy, site integrity, contributor review |
| Contributors | Proposals via pull request under [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) |

BIU.G Academy operates in connection with Biu-g Holdings LLC. Corporate governance details beyond education mission are **not** published in this repository.

---

## Future advisory model (planned)

As programs scale, the initiative may add:

- Education quality advisors
- Curriculum subject reviewers
- Technical architecture advisors
- Community moderation leads
- External regulatory counsel (as needed)

Advisors inform decisions; they do not automatically imply institutional endorsement on the public website.

## Educational integrity principles

1. No false credential claims by staff, contributors, or partners
2. No guaranteed employment, income, or investment outcomes
3. Distinguish training certificates from accredited degrees unless lawfully established
4. Protect learners from exploitation and predatory financial messaging
5. Human oversight for admissions and sensitive AI assist (when deployed)

---

## Documentation standards

- Institutional intent lives in `docs/` and root policy files
- **Operational** vs **under development** vs **planned** must be explicit
- White paper and README stay aligned on regulatory statements
- Material changes update [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md)

Index: [docs/README.md](docs/README.md)

---

## Decision-making principles

1. **Accuracy over speed** — Public claims match capability
2. **Regulatory caution** — No implied MESCTI/INAAREES approval or government partnership
3. **Privacy by default** — Minimize data; no secrets in git
4. **Learner safety** — Code of conduct enforced
5. **Long-term thinking** — Infrastructure before hype
